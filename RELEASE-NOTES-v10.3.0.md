# NileStock v10.3.0 — Launch & Growth release

## Commercial
- New customer-facing **Lite** tier at UGX 9,500/month.
- Business adjusted to UGX 49,500/month and Pro to UGX 99,500/month.
- Free remains UGX 0.
- Annual billing remains equivalent to paying for 10 months.
- Free: 10 products; Lite: 100 products; Business/Pro: unlimited.
- Software subscriptions do not include physical POS hardware unless sold separately.

## Offline and device experience
- Cloud sessions now persist a safe local session reference and business-specific workspace backup.
- Offline work is backed up locally even while cloud sync is unavailable.
- Reconnection triggers cloud rehydration and existing sale sync automatically.
- New service worker separates shell/static caches and caches Next static assets for more reliable offline starts.
- Branded startup screen replaces a blank loading screen.
- Existing stable camera scanner retained; experimental iPhone camera changes are not included.

## SEO and brand
- Primary domain prepared as `https://nilestock.shop`.
- Rich metadata, canonical URL, Open Graph/Twitter card, sitemap, robots, SoftwareApplication schema and FAQ schema.
- Privacy and Terms pages.
- PWA PNG icons and Apple touch icon.
- Premium social share image.
- Landing copy now explains phone use, offline behavior, auto-generated barcode/QR codes and optional hardware.
