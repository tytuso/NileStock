# NileStock pricing & market positioning

## Recommended launch ladder

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (2 months free) | Best for |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free | UGX 0 | Free | Testing / very small counter |
| Lite | UGX 9,500 | UGX 95,000 | Micro & small shops |
| Business | UGX 49,500 | UGX 495,000 | Growing shops, minimarts, teams |
| Pro | UGX 99,500 | UGX 995,000 | Serious retailers needing AI/advanced control |

The internal database ID for Lite remains `starter`, so existing Supabase constraints and founder controls stay compatible.

## Why keep UGX 9,500

The low paid tier gives a shop owner an easy first purchase without forcing NileStock to compete only as a free product. It also creates a clear upgrade path: 10 products on Free, 100 on Lite, unlimited on Business/Pro.

Do not put every advanced feature into Lite. Keep staff control, supplier/purchase workflows, customer credit, branded PDF reports and operational exports as Business value. Keep the AI Adviser exclusive to Pro.

## Hardware position

NileStock is software-first. Customers can begin with a phone, tablet or laptop they already own. Hardware should be optional:

- Receipt printer — separate purchase
- Cash drawer — separate purchase
- USB/Bluetooth barcode scanner — optional separate purchase
- Complete POS terminal — optional separate purchase

Later, sell a **NileStock Setup Bundle** or partner with a hardware supplier. That lets the subscription stay affordable while creating a second revenue stream from setup, hardware, training and support.

## Barcode / QR message for customers

A shop can use the barcode already printed on a product. If the product has no barcode, leave the field blank and NileStock generates a local product code and QR value automatically. Lite and higher plans can download/print code sheets.

## Offline message

Use precise wording: **“Works offline after first setup. Pending changes sync automatically when internet returns.”** Do not promise that a first-ever sign-in or first-ever installation works with zero internet.

## Domain

Launch on **nilestock.shop**. Use the apex domain for the current combined landing page + app. Redirect `www.nilestock.shop` to the apex. Do not split to `app.nilestock.shop` until the marketing site and authenticated app are truly separate deployments.
